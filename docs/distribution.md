## Copy-ready catalog listing

**Name:** Business Idea Stress Test

**Short summary:** Evidence-first, open-source AI skill that interrogates business ideas, researches demand and competitors, models unit economics, challenges optimistic assumptions, and designs lean tests before founders commit capital

**Long description:** Business Idea Stress Test provides a structured, one-off, six-stage workflow for pre-investment business and startup idea validation. It interviews the founder, pressure-tests the core hypothesis, gathers traceable market and customer evidence when browsing is available, evaluates financial and operational assumptions, makes the strongest countercase, and produces a conditional decision brief with measurable pilot criteria. Written in the portable Agent Skills format with installation guidance for ChatGPT, Codex, Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Cursor, GitHub Copilot and other compatible hosts. No paid API is required to load the skill; external research quality depends on host tools and evidence access
