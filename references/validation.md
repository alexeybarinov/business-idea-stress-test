# Stage 2 — Early idea validation

**Methodological inspiration:** BuildGreatProducts' Idea Validator. Look for the reasons a venture may fail **before** commissioning broad research, purchasing infrastructure, or writing a business plan.

## Core hypothesis

Write the hypothesis as a falsifiable statement:

> Segment X experiences problem Y with frequency Z; those buyers will switch from alternative A and pay P for outcome R through channel C

Keep unknown variables visible. Do not fill them with plausible-sounding invented values.

## Five failure dimensions

1. **Desirability:** the problem is consequential enough to motivate action
2. **Willingness to pay:** buyers have budgets and have demonstrated payment behavior or can be tested for it
3. **Distribution:** buyers are reachable through realistic channels at feasible cost
4. **Economic feasibility:** enough contribution remains after delivery, acquisition, overhead, and refunds
5. **Legal and operational feasibility:** the promise can lawfully and reliably be delivered in the target location

Map all realistic substitutes: incumbent vendors, DIY, informal solutions, legacy workflows, and choosing to do nothing. “I cannot find a competitor” does not prove an uncontested opportunity.

Identify **3–7 critical assumptions**, each with its potential counterargument, consequence of being wrong, earliest observable disconfirming evidence, and cheapest ethical test. Prioritize potential fatal legal or economic blockers; assess applicability from dated primary sources when possible. Legal concerns requiring specialist review are questions, not confident legal rulings.

## Challenge questions

- Would this target segment even use the product if it were free? If not, pricing is probably not the central uncertainty
- What existing method achieves most of the desired job with lower switching costs?
- Why would customers change behavior **now**, rather than postpone or keep the status quo?
- What specific observation would falsify the core hypothesis?

Do not assign arbitrary scores or predict a precise chance of success. Report the evidence state as **supported**, **contradicted**, **disputed**, or **not yet tested**.

### Deliverables

One explicit core hypothesis, a ranked-by-consequence assumption register (not invented numeric scoring), a fast blocker check, and the next research questions.
