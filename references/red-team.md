# Stage 5 — Adversarial review / Devil's Advocate

**Methodological inspiration:** jukeyman's Devil's Advocate. Do not rubber-stamp earlier conclusions because they have already appeared in the working report. First present the strongest defensible **case in favor**, then make a serious attempt to falsify it using the best available counterevidence.

## Separate analytical lenses

1. **Buyer:** Why abandon the current solution? Why not defer the purchase or do the job manually? Does switching impose money, time, reputation, or trust costs?
2. **Incumbent competitor:** Why do buyers stay with present providers? Can those providers replicate the proposed difference quickly, lower prices, or block practical distribution channels?
3. **Finance:** Which 1–3 inputs make the financial case fragile? Have GMV and earned revenue been confused, or revenue and cash flow, or leads and paying customers?
4. **Operations:** What fails at 10, 100, or 1000 orders? Check bottlenecks in fulfillment, supplier coordination, service quality, chargebacks, consumer obligations, returns, and seasonal capacity
5. **Regulatory:** Identify jurisdiction-specific questions about permissions, customer terms, personal data, payments, labor, and liability **only to the extent evidence supports them**. Flag questions needing expert legal review; do not hallucinate a legal verdict

These are **perspectives simulated by a single assistant**, not a real meeting, real named experts, or consensus among independent models.

## Twelve-month pre-mortem

Assume, as a deliberately hypothetical exercise, that the venture failed within twelve months. Provide five *specific plausible failure hypotheses*, not factual predictions. For each include:

- earliest detectable warning sign
- failure mechanism and material consequence
- what evidence would support or contradict it
- cheapest appropriate counterexperiment
- a proposed stop/pivot condition

## Steelman the disagreement

Document the strongest case *for* and the strongest case *against*, identify the exact unresolved variable that makes them diverge, then propose the smallest real-world observation that could resolve that disagreement.

Never invent quotations from customers or purported experts. Criticism is an investigative technique, not a goal in itself: acknowledge genuine strengths and confirmed mitigating evidence.

### Deliverables

Threats grouped by **material consequence and testability**, a table of substantive disagreements and missing evidence, a failure pre-mortem, and leading indicators worth monitoring *during the test*, not recurring AI meetings.
