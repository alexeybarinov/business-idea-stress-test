# Stage 6 — Final decision brief and bounded test

Do not imply research is complete while consequential evidence is missing. Begin with a **short conditional conclusion**, then provide auditable supporting analysis. If the core hypothesis remains untested, say so explicitly.

## Required final report structure

1. **Idea brief:** offer, customer/payer, geography, business model, founder goal, constraints, and starting budget
2. **Conditional assessment:** grounds to test / revise before testing / material reasons not to launch in this form / insufficient evidence. State exactly what future observations could change that assessment
3. **Evidence and uncertainty:** critical corroborated facts, accurately labeled founder statements, external estimates, conflicting findings, and gaps. No invented numeric chance of success
4. **Market and customers:** target segments, current substitutes, evidence of real demand, TAM/SAM/SOM only with defensible inputs
5. **Competition:** direct alternatives, indirect alternatives, and status quo; comparable pricing where documented; differentiation hypothesis and switching barriers
6. **Financial and operational model:** sourced inputs, worked formulas, conditional scenarios, initial cash requirements, capacity limits, and sensitivity
7. **Risk and adversarial review:** strongest case for/against, five plausible failure hypotheses, and actionable warning signs
8. **Reality check:** the lowest-cost feasible sequential experiment under relevant jurisdictional requirements. A 7–14-day test may be appropriate; otherwise justify a different horizon
9. **First-stage action plan:** prerequisite activities, verified permit questions, contractual preparation, outreach channels, test offer, and measurement plan
10. **Remaining founder questions:** ask only those that would materially change the action plan or assessment
11. **Evidence ledger and source list:** URLs, publication/data/access dates, geography, limitations. Put citations next to claims as well as in the ledger

## Lean experiment protocol

For **each** proposed experiment, define:

- hypothesis and at least one competing explanation
- observable **real customer action**: payment, compliant pre-order, genuine qualified inquiry at an explicit price, or a paid trial agreement, as appropriate
- target segment, ethical contact method, and data/privacy considerations; never propose unsolicited spam or fake reviews
- experiment window, accountable owner, required resources, and **budget ceiling approved by the founder before any spending**
- sample-size limitations and what cannot be inferred statistically from a small test
- success signal, unambiguous stop condition, and decision triggered by positive/negative/ambiguous results

Do not treat ad clicks without transactions as proof of willingness to pay. Do not propose a costly full launch when a limited experiment can test the central assumption.

## Evidence ledger format

| ID | Claim / assumption | F/E/C/H/U | Source URL or founder input / explicit model inputs | Date + geography | Disconfirming test | Decision impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

F/E/C/H/U definitions are in `SKILL.md`. Do not confuse a founder's accurate statement about their own resources (F, founder-provided) with independently verified external market facts (F, externally verified).

## If the conversation pauses

Append a **handoff card** containing: two-line idea summary; confirmed founder-supplied facts; stages completed; open questions; top three risk hypotheses; key sources already established; and the exact next stage/action. Never claim the assistant will continue researching offline. Suggest pasting the card into a new conversation when necessary.
